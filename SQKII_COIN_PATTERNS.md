# Sqkii "Hunt The Mouse" — Past Coin Finds, Rules & Heuristics

A research report compiling every publicly documented Sqkii Hunt The Mouse coin find in
Singapore, the rules that constrain where coins can be hidden, and practical heuristics
derived from verified finds and community discussion. All claims below survived a
3-vote adversarial verification pass against the cited sources (verified as of July 2026).

---

## 1. Documented past finds

Only **three physical gold-coin hiding spots plus one virtual hide** are publicly documented
in enough detail to be verifiable. Silver-coin finds are only anecdotally documented.

## 2. The strongest predictive filter: Sqkii's own rules

The official FAQ (huntthemouse.sqkii.com/faq, restated across editions and in CEO Kenny
Choy's on-record responses) is the best negative filter. Coins are **never** placed where finding them requires:

- Trespassing into private property or restricted areas
- Swimming or diving into water bodies
- Climbing into sewage canals or onto roofs/high platforms
- Bashing through thick forestry
- Entering extremely dark, unlit places
- Walking onto roads
- Digging, or breaking open / dismantling anything

⚠️ Read "publicly accessible" as **"no locked or restricted access"**, not "public land only" —
the 2019 coin sat on a roadside *inside a private landed estate* (the street itself is walkable).
Recent editions also publish explicit exclusion zones (e.g. Sentosa 2025 excluded Sentosa Cove,
RWS, and places of worship).

100% of documented finds are consistent with these rules: ground-level, unenclosed,
reachable without any barrier.

## 3. Location-selection heuristics (derived from verified finds)

Confidence: **medium** — generalized from only 3 physical + 1 virtual gold find.

1. **Ground level, waist height or below.** Every physical gold coin was under/beside a tree or bench. Micro-spots: bench undersides wedged into structure, tree-base nooks, behind pillars or pipes (per Mothership's characterization during the 2024 hunt), under walls along staircases, behind fences (reported silver finds).
2. **Light camouflage only.** A rock placed on top (2026), wedged into a plank gap (2024) — never buried, never inside anything you'd have to dismantle.
3. **Locations alternate between two archetypes:**
   - Dense HDB heartland precincts (Bedok Central, in front of a FairPrice block), and
   - Quiet, low-footfall fringe streets (Jalan Naung private estate; Tagore Road industrial lane) — quiet enough for Sqkii to hide the coin unobserved, but never access-restricted.
4. **24/7 accessibility is a real constraint.** Two of three physical gold finds happened at night (1:43 am, 11:32 pm) — the spots had to be searchable at those hours, and after-hours grinding is the documented winning pattern.
5. **Islandwide heartland spread for silver coins.** 2024 launch-day silvers went to Punggol, Yishun, Tampines, Sengkang, Pasir Ris, Clementi — residential towns, not central/tourist areas.
6. **Overlooked non-residential lanes are in play** (Tagore Road) — don't restrict searches to residential estates.

## 4. Clue-decoding patterns

1. **Individual clues are weak signals; only full-set convergence is reliable.** In 2018–19, Mothership itself mapped the released clues convincingly onto Sin Ming/Bright Hill Rd in Bishan — nowhere near the actual Hougang spot. Confirmation bias toward a plausible area is the single biggest failure mode. Community verdict on Sqkii's Facebook: clues were "useless at best, and misleading, at worst."
2. **Decisive decodes reward lateral wordplay tied to physical public signage/infrastructure.** The 2022 winning decode: "Sqkii was reminded that sharing is caring" → Park Connector Network, because PCN signs remind users it's a *shared path*. A forum-cited earlier example: "those look like animals" = port cranes.
3. **Ground-truth on foot beats armchair mapping.** The 2022 winners walked the entire PCN matching the remaining hints against real locations.
4. **Clue cadence is staged and predictable:** roughly 3 hints/day (92 clues over the 2018–19 hunt; 10 am/2 pm/6 pm on Instagram/Facebook/Telegram in 2024), plus physical clue ads (MRT trains on the NS and Circle lines in 2018–19).
5. **Study Sqkii's recap clips** (TikTok/Facebook @sqkiimouse) of past finds for hiding-style signatures — the pillars/pipes pattern comes from these.
6. **Shrinking-circle mechanic (2019 edition):** each coin sits in a map circle that shrinks at published times (down to ~50 m), with prize decaying through seven tiers (S$5,000 → $3,000 → $1,500 → $1,000 → $500 → $200 → $100), colour-coded. Earlier find = bigger payout; plan searches around scheduled shrink times. (Mechanics change per edition.)

## 5. Timing patterns

- **Hunt windows:** gold-coin hunts ran ~9–34 days from drop to find. The 2024 edition ran 30 days (Oct 10 – Nov 9), gold dropped inside a **pre-announced window** (Oct 28–Nov 3; actually hidden Oct 30).
- **Staged drops:** 2024 had 25 silver coins on launch day, the remaining 275 released progressively over 30 days (S$1M pool: 1× S$500k gold + 175× S$2,500 + 125× S$500 silvers). S$74,500 in silvers was found within the first four days (operator-reported figure).
- **Winning finds happen at night.** Sustained round-the-clock team searching immediately after the gold drop is the documented winning pattern.
- **Verification lag:** the SG60 win took ~2 days of formal verification (including filmed proof of the finder replacing the coin and panning surroundings) before announcement.

## 6. Community strategy consensus & controversies

- **Teams win.** Every documented gold find was by an organized team (3–9 people) with pre-agreed splits, often coordinated via Telegram. The 2019 duo won again in 2022 — experience compounds.
- **Main controversy: clue ambiguity.** Deliberately vague clues that map onto multiple plausible areas (the Mothership Sin Ming episode; "useless at best, misleading at worst" complaints; "drips and drabs" release pacing criticism).
- **Property-damage concerns:** a Stomp report raised concerns about hunters damaging property; Sqkii's CEO responded on record pointing to the no-trespass/no-dismantling rules.
- **Caveat on coverage:** several Mothership articles about Sqkii are brand-partnered; aggregate find figures come from Sqkii itself.
- **Forum sweep result:** HardwareZone EDMW threads exist for most editions (hint-swapping, area-elimination), but no specific Reddit/HardwareZone/Telegram strategy claim survived adversarial verification — community consensus above rests mainly on verified winner interviews and news-documented complaints.

---

## 7. Practical playbook (synthesis)

1. **Filter first by the safety rules** — eliminate water, heights, forests, locked/restricted areas, dark places, anything requiring digging or dismantling. This shrinks the viable search space enormously.
2. **Search the micro-spots:** underside of benches (gaps between planks and ledges), tree-base nooks (check under small rocks), behind pillars and pipes, along staircase walls and fences — waist height or below, in plain public view.
3. **Weight both archetypes:** HDB precinct furniture *and* quiet fringe/industrial lanes near, but not in, major corridors.
4. **Don't lock onto one area early.** Hold multiple candidate areas until the full clue set converges; Sqkii's clues are designed to fit wrong locations.
5. **Decode clues against public signage wording** (PCN signs, estate signboards, infrastructure plaques), then walk the candidate corridors physically.
6. **Form a team with a written split agreement**, divide the map, and be prepared to grind nights immediately after the gold-coin drop window opens.
7. **Watch Sqkii's channels at hint times** (historically 10 am/2 pm/6 pm) and study their past-find recap videos for hiding signatures.

---

## Caveats & open questions

- Sample size is small (3 physical gold hides + 1 virtual); Sqkii changes mechanics every edition, so timing/mechanic patterns are time-sensitive.
- The 2016 inaugural (S$50,000) hiding spot is undocumented; the actual SG60 winning clue chain is undocumented.
- Silver-coin hides (hundreds per edition) and sponsor editions (ShopBack, Sentosa 2025, DBS SG60) lack verifiable find records that would enlarge the pattern sample.
- Live-player Telegram/EDMW tactics (grid-search coordination, hint-triangulation sheets) could not be verified in this pass.
