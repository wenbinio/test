# It's the Law (Says Who?): storyboard

One row per sung line. Times get filled in once the Suno track exists and its lyrics are timed; shot lengths then follow the song, roughly 2–3 s a line. The **Out** column is how each shot hands over to the next.

## The idea

Clawd rides up to a park gate. The sign says no vehicles, the Keeper blows a whistle ("It's the law!") and Clawd asks the question every law student gets to eventually: *says who?*

The question turns into a climb. Each chorus follows one answer: the king with a stick, the constitution in the clouds, conscience, the courts, Hercules. Each is higher and stranger than the last, until "let justice be done though the heavens fall" brings the whole painted sky down. Clawd lands back at the gate at sunset. The answer was standing there all along: everyone who keeps the rule, Clawd included. Clawd gets off the bike and walks it through.

- **Logline.** Clawd wants to ride into the park, but "it's the law", so Clawd climbs the whole of legal philosophy to find out who says so, and finds the answer back at the gate.
- **World.** One storybook world, stacked vertically: the park at ground level; a throne room; a staircase of books into the clouds; a city wall at night; a courtroom in the clouds; Hercules' library at the top; a philosophy funhouse in space; the falling sky; the park at sunset.
- **Motif.** Clawd's "?" emote. It grows every chorus (pea → head → Clawd → building), and in the last line Clawd hangs it on the sign.
- **Clawd's arc.** Curious → smitten → confused → dizzy → moved → sceptical → hopeful → overwhelmed → content.
- **Rules for every shot** (from the ClaudeAnimationBase guide): something happens; one read at a time; everything moves on the beat; a transition at every seam. The only text is the karaoke captions and the name tags.

## What ties it together

- **Same gate at the start and the end.** Clawd rides up in the first shot and walks through in the last.
- **Every chorus opens the same way:** a whistle, the Keeper points at the sign, Clawd's "?" pops, bigger each time. This is our version of P(doom)'s rising meter.
- **One climb.** Verse 2 builds a staircase out of rulebooks. Everything after it is further up, until the sky falls.
- **Name tags.** When a thinker's line is sung, a small painted tag with their name pops up beside them and leaves with them.
- **Captions.** Every sung line runs in a karaoke bar along the bottom, so nothing important is staged below y ≈ 960. The same timings export as an .srt caption file.
- **Palette arc.** Morning greens and cream → royal crimson and gold → sky blues turning white → night indigo and candle ochre → courtroom browns and breakfast yellow → hero gold and library teal → funhouse violet and rose → every colour falling → sunset gold.

| Lyric | Shot | Out |

| Who says? The Grundnorm. We assume it, that's all. | The top step is just a cloud. Clawd hesitates, shrugs, steps on, and it holds, but only while Clawd nods. When Clawd frowns, it sags. Tag: *Kelsen*. | Clawd looks down: vertigo |
| Rex's rules were secret, vague, and new each day, | Next door is a wobbly palace. King Rex locks the rules in a chest (secret), scribbles a smudged sign (vague) and flips a calendar that swaps the sign on every page (new each day). Tag: *Fuller*. | Pages flick into the next gag |
| retroactive, clashing, and impossible to obey. | Rex fines a Clawd while a clock spins backwards (retroactive). Two arrows on one post point opposite ways (clashing). A rule orders everyone to jump to the moon: they try, and flop (impossible). | Everyone lands in a heap |

Fuller's other two failures run as background gags in these shots: Rex deciding each case with a coin flip (no general rules), and Rex strolling past his own "keep off the grass" sign across the grass (officials ignoring the rules).
