# Singapore RLT — where to send an application

Relevant Legal Training: the six months an overseas Scheduled University graduate serves before
becoming a qualified person. **RLT and the practice training contract are different applications** —
many firms run two separate listings.

`RLT (6 mths) → Part A → Part B → PTP (12 mths) → admission`

Open `index.html` for the filterable version. Checked **31 July 2026**; live cycle is RLT 2027 / PTC 2028.

## Rules that decide whether the six months counts

- **6 months inside a continuous 8-month window**, in full or half days. Weekends and public holidays count toward the period but cannot be the start or end date.
- **Finish before Part B begins** — by the last working day before the course starts. Leave must be made up at the end, pushing your finish date. Leave a buffer.
- **Paralegal or internship work does not count** unless formalised as a supervised training arrangement and certified by the practice. Arrange it in writing at the start, never retroactively.
- **No SILE registration beforehand**, but you must submit a certification of completion on SILE's template at the end. Check the firm knows it will sign one.
- **You can stitch periods together** across organisations, so long as the total hits six months in the eight-month window.
- **The 1 July moratorium applies to practice training contracts, not RLT.** If a firm says wait, ask which they mean.
- **Pay is unregulated.** No mandated minimum; Guidance Note 3.9.1 asks only that basic transport and meal costs be covered. Lee & Lee's $3,000/month is a fair large-firm benchmark. The term of art is *honorarium*.

## If nothing above fits

- **Pupillage in England, Wales or Scotland** — as pupil of, or reading in the chambers of, a barrister or Scottish advocate of 7+ years' standing.
- **A foreign law practice** — supervised training in foreign law under a formal training arrangement. A UK or Australian training contract can do double duty if documented and certifiable.
- **Speculative approaches work.** A six-month seat is cheaper for a firm to say yes to than a training contract. Firms that train but publish no trainee page: RHTLaw Asia, Oon & Bazul, Tan Kok Quan Partnership, Wee Swee Teow, PDLegal, Providence Law Asia, Setia Law, Duane Morris & Selvam, Morgan Lewis Stamford, Pinsent Masons MPillay, Ashurst ADTLaw, CMS Holborn Asia, Clyde & Co Clasis, HFW, Watson Farley & Williams, Reed Smith / Resource Law.
- **Search** [Law Society job search](https://lawsocietycareers.com.sg/search-jobs/) for "relevant legal training" — some practices post outside the training-contract section.
