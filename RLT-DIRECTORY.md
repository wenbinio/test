## 0. Read this first — RLT is not the training contract

This is the most common mix-up, and it changes which form you fill in.

| | **RLT** | **PTP / PTC** |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 6 months | 12 months |
| Who needs it | Overseas Scheduled University graduates only | Everyone, local and overseas |
| What it gets you | *Qualified person* status | Admission as an advocate and solicitor |
| When | Before the Part B course starts | After Part B |

Overseas-graduate sequence: **RLT → Part A → Part B → 12-month PTP → call to the Bar.**

Several firms run **two separate application listings** for these (Rajah & Tann, Drew & Napier,
Kennedys). Apply to the right one.

**Core rules (SILE):**

- 6 months within a continuous **8-month period**, counted in full or half days only.
- Typically commences around **August**.
- Must be completed by the **last working day before the Part B course begins**.
- **No SILE registration** needed before starting — but you must submit a written certification of
  completion from the training organisation at the end (template on SILE's Resources page).
- May be served before, during or after the Part A examinations.
- May be **accumulated across more than one organisation**.

Sources: [SILE — Relevant Legal Training](https://www.sile.edu.sg/the-singapore-bar-admission/relevant-legal-training/) ·
[SILE — RLT FAQs](https://www.sile.edu.sg/faqs-relevant-legal-training)

## 5. The three routes that aren't a Singapore law firm

SILE recognises four categories of RLT. Most applicants only consider the first.

1. **Singapore law practice** — everything in sections 1–4 above.
2. **Pupillage in England, Wales or Scotland** — training as a pupil, or reading in the chambers of, a
   barrister or Scottish advocate of at least **7 years' standing**.
3. **A foreign law practice** — supervised training in the practice of foreign law under a **formal
   training arrangement**. A UK, Australian or Malaysian training contract or seat can do double duty,
   but it must be documented and certifiable, not a summer job.
4. **Public service and in-house** — as a Judicial or Legal Service Officer, or under a **Qualifying
   Relevant Legal Officer** (advocate and solicitor of at least 5 years' standing).

> **In-house limit — check before you commit.** SILE's FAQs indicate that for relevant legal work the
> employer must be **listed on an official securities exchange**; a private limited company generally
> does not qualify. Get written confirmation from SILE for your specific employer before spending six
> months on it. [Contact SILE](https://www.sile.edu.sg/contact-us/)

## 7. How to send one

The pack is close to identical across firms. Build it once, tailor the cover letter.

1. **One PDF, correctly named.** Several firms require the whole application in a single file — Rajah &
   Tann caps it at 12MB. Name it `Surname_FirstName_RLT2027.pdf`.
2. **Cover letter, one page.** Preferred practice areas and why that firm. Where seat preferences are
   requested, generally no more than two.
3. **CV, up to three pages.** Predicted classification and current GPA/CAP. Several Singapore firms
   expect academic history from secondary school onwards (O-levels, A-levels or equivalent); Drew &
   Napier asks for a photograph and nationality.
4. **Transcripts.** Latest available, including the term immediately preceding your application.
   Unofficial is normally fine at application stage.
5. **Say which thing you are applying for.** Put "Relevant Legal Training" and the year in the subject
   line or first sentence. Virtus Law specifies the exact subject-line format.
6. **Keep the certification in mind from day one.** You must submit a written certification of
   completion on SILE's template. Confirm before accepting that the firm knows it will sign one.

## 8. Things worth knowing

- **The moratorium does not bite on RLT.** The 1 July gate applies to *practice training contracts*,
  not RLT. Lee & Lee states this explicitly. If a firm tells you to wait, ask whether they mean the TC
  or the RLT — several will take RLT applications off-cycle.
- **The moratorium rule itself.** If you are eligible to start Part B in July 2027 or later, you may
  apply for a PTC no earlier than one year before the first day of the month of the earliest Part B
  session you qualify for. Exempt if you are already a Qualified Person or an Eligible Person;
  grandfathered if you secured a contract before 1 October 2024 for a January 2028 or later start.
  [SILE — Practice Training Period](https://www.sile.edu.sg/the-singapore-bar-admission/practice-training-period/)
- **Paralegal and internship work does not count** unless formalised as a supervised training
  arrangement and certified as such by the practice. Get it in writing at the start; it does not
  convert retroactively.
- **You can stitch periods together** across more than one organisation, so long as the total reaches
  six months inside a continuous eight-month window.
- **Weekends and public holidays count toward the period** but cannot be your start or end date. Leave
  is deducted in half-day and full-day units and must be made up at the end, pushing your completion
  date. Plan a buffer before Part B rather than finishing to the day.
- **Pay varies enormously and is not regulated.** The Law Society deliberately declined to mandate a
  minimum; Guidance Note 3.9.1 asks only that a practice cover basic transport and meal expenses.
  Lee & Lee's published $3,000/month is a reasonable large-firm benchmark. The term of art is
  **honorarium**, not salary or allowance.
- **An RLT seat is not an offer of a training contract.** At most firms these are separate decisions
  with separate rounds — though performing well in RLT is the best route into that firm's TC. Ask
  where the firm stands before accepting.
- **Public service trainees have extra machinery.** The framework applying from 1 January 2026 sets a
  12-month PTP, requires a mandatory three-month attachment at AGC or a Singapore law practice for
  those supervised by a QRLO, and requires SILE approval of the manner of service **at least 14 days
  before** training starts. Miss that window and the period served may not count.
- **PTP itself is now 12 months** and must be completed within a continuous 16-month period, based in
  Singapore throughout.
