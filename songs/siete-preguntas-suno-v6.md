# Ocho Preguntas: a Suno v6 song that teaches Spanish question words

**What it teaches:** the 8 core Spanish question words (qué, quién, dónde, cuándo, cómo, cuánto, cuál, por qué), each in a real sentence, plus two common beginner mistakes:
- `¿Cómo te llamas?` means "What's your name?" even though `cómo` means "how".
- `por qué` (two words, with an accent) means "why". `porque` (one word, no accent) means "because".

**How the song teaches:** the chorus pairs each Spanish word with its English meaning, and the verses put every word into a sentence you can actually use. In the final chorus the English is removed so you fill it in yourself.

## Generation notes

- Spanish accents (á, é, ñ, ¿) are left in on purpose. They help the model pronounce the words correctly, so don't strip them.
- Lines in `(parentheses)` are usually sung as backing or echo vocals. That's how the English glosses stay audible without breaking the melody.
- If the Spanish comes out with an English accent, add `native Spanish-speaking vocalist` to the start of the Style field and regenerate.
- If one section comes out wrong (for example, the Final Chorus doesn't do call-and-response), fix that section with v6's section editing instead of regenerating the whole song.
- The `[Short pause]` in the outro only sometimes produces a real gap. Treat it as a bonus, not something you can count on.
