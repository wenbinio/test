Tracker

Track your bladder and bowel habits

A week of honest notes tells a doctor more than trying to remember it all in the waiting room. Log a pee, a poo or a drink in a few seconds, and this page will add up the totals, show your week at a glance, and point out anything worth raising. Everything you type stays in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.

Pattern check

This reads your saved entries and points out anything that crosses a commonly used threshold. It is a prompt to have a conversation, not a diagnosis. It cannot see you, and it only knows what you have logged.

Some things do not wait for a pattern. Blood in your urine or stool, black tarry stool, being unable to pass urine at all, or fever with back or side pain all need attention now rather than after another week of logging. Read the red flags page.

Summary for your doctor

This builds a plain-text summary of your log — the date range, daily counts, the spread of Bristol types, any blood entries and any flags raised. Read it over first, then copy it into an email, a notes app, or print it out. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no download file.

  • Say how long the pattern has been going on, not just what this week looked like.
  • Mention any medicines you have started or stopped recently, including supplements.
  • Say plainly if anything has changed: a new pattern matters more than an unusual one.

Manage this log

“Add a sample week” fills the tracker with made-up entries so you can see how it behaves. They are labelled sample data in the table above and in any summary you build, and “Remove sample data” takes them out again without touching anything you logged yourself. “Delete all data” wipes everything and asks you to confirm first; it cannot be undone.

Where is my data actually stored?

In your browser's local storage, under a single key on this site. It never travels over the network. It is not encrypted, so anyone with access to your unlocked device and this browser could open this page and read it.

Why does the page say it cannot save?

Private or incognito windows, and some privacy settings, block a page from storing anything. The tracker still works for the current session, but the log disappears when you close the tab. Build the summary and copy it out before you go.

How long should I log for?

Three days gives a doctor something useful; seven days is better, because it covers a weekend and a working week. Longer than a fortnight is rarely needed unless you have been asked for it.

Do I have to log everything?

No. A log with only pees, or only bowel motions, is still useful. Just remember that the totals and the pattern check can only count what you actually enter — a low fluid total may mean you drank little, or simply that you did not log your drinks.

This site is general health information, not medical advice.

It cannot examine you, does not know your history, and is no substitute for a clinician who can. If a symptom is listed here as urgent, treat it as urgent. If something feels badly wrong and this site does not mention it, that is a reason to get seen, not a reason to wait.
