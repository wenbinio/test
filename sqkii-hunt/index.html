      How this stays inside the rules

      Everything here is inference over public information — the exclusions Sqkii publishes, the clues released on their socials, and where past coins were reported found. It reads nothing from any SQKII app, endpoint, or account, and can't: the value is in reasoning, not access. Coastline, exclusion areas and past-find pins are hand-placed approximations for thinking, not survey-grade coordinates.
